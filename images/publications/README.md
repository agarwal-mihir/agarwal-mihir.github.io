# Publication illustrations

The SVGs in this directory are original concept illustrations made for this
website. They illustrate the topic or method of each paper; they are not
experimental plots, reproduced paper figures, or measurements. The publication
page labels them "Concept illustration" and supplies descriptive alternative
text. No third-party figures are included.

Concepts are grounded in the linked publications:

- `linear-corrections.svg`: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14196
- `multivectors.svg`: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21494
- `single-vector.svg`: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29519
- `ultrasound.svg`: https://doi.org/10.3389/facou.2026.1882624
- `geomagnetic.svg`: https://doi.org/10.1109/SPACE65882.2025.11171320
- `veribench.svg`: https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCAS56072.2025.11044004
- `peptides.svg`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=FKCYEbSaE1
- `rainfall.svg`: https://www.climatechange.ai/papers/neurips2023/115
- `air-quality.svg`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=7juV7SKVvM
- `stations.svg`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=I1yGbIPGjx
- `conformal.svg`: https://github.com/agarwal-mihir/Conformal-Prediction

The rainfall workshop paper and its later arXiv version are one record. Related
air-quality workshop papers retain their separate titles. The retrieval papers
are labeled as preprints, and the ultrasound study is described as an ex vivo
proof of concept.
